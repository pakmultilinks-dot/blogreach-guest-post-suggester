# Output Quality Review: 10 Niches

The tool was run for 10 niches. Each returned exactly 10 ideas with no
near-duplicates inside a niche. Honest assessment below, including the weak
results.

## Niches tested

1. Personal finance - strong. All 10 angles fit naturally.
2. SaaS marketing - strong. Acronym fix renders "SaaS" correctly.
3. Home gardening - good, with one weak angle (see below).
4. Pet care - good. Practical angles fit well.
5. Electric vehicles - weakest of the set (see below).
6. Wedding planning - good, with one weak angle.
7. Cybersecurity - strong. Ethics and automation angles fit naturally.
8. Yoga and wellness - good. Casing fix keeps "and" lowercase.
9. Real estate investing - strong.
10. Coffee culture - good, with one weak angle.
11. AI in education - strong after the A/An and acronym fixes ("An AI in
    Education Roadmap", not "A Ai").

## Weak results (honest notes)

1. **Plural niches break verb agreement.** "How Electric Vehicles Is Changing"
   should read "Are Changing". The template assumes a singular niche. Fix would
   need plural detection.
2. **"vs. the Alternatives" is a poor fit for product niches.** It works for
   practices (SaaS marketing vs. alternatives) but reads oddly for electric
   vehicles, where the real comparison is against petrol cars, which the
   template cannot know.
3. **"Metrics That Matter" is a weak fit for hobby and lifestyle niches.**
   "How to Measure Success in Home Gardening" and the wedding-planning version
   are the least clickable ideas in their lists.
4. **"Automation" is a weak fit for culture niches.** "Coffee Culture
   Automation" is strained; the angle suits business and tech niches only.
5. **Angles repeat across niches.** Within one niche the 10 ideas are diverse,
   but run two niches side by side and the same 25 templates are visible. Fine
   for ideation, not a substitute for editorial judgment.
6. **No demand data.** Ideas are generated from headline patterns, not from
   search volume or audience research. A human should sanity-check the final
   picks before pitching.

## Verdict

Good enough as a first-draft ideation tool for BlogReach writers: it reliably
produces 10 varied, pitchable starting points per niche in under a second.
The weak spots are angle-to-niche fit (points 2 to 4) and plural grammar
(point 1), both fixable with a per-angle niche-type filter in a future pass.
